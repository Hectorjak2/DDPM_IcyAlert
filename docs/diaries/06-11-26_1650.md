# 06-11-26:16:50

**Focus:** Why the conditional DDPM can't get both melt and cool season right, and whether the noise schedule is to blame (it isn't).

## Theory
- **The network is fine; the sampling chain drifts.** On `ddpm-titanfull-shif64`, the x0 estimate from pure noise (t=999) has the right sign in both seasons: +0.15 vs +0.23 true in Nov/Dec, correlation 0.82–0.90 with the true change. Swapping the month flips the sign. With x_t from `q_sample` at t=400 it gives +0.21 vs +0.23 (correlation 0.98). Inside the 1000-step chain on 2024-11-19, the marginal-ice-zone estimate decays from +0.226 to −0.014 (truth +0.312).
- **Schedule tuning only moves the drift, so it looked like a melt-vs-cool trade-off.** `titanfull-shif64` drifts toward melt (cool-season mean predicted −0.056 vs +0.107 true). `originalC` drifts the other way (melt +0.018 vs −0.096). Whichever season matches the drift direction looks good.
- **No single schedule shift suits one season better.** In every month the residual keeps over 90% of its energy at 8×8 block averages and 50–90% at 64×64, so the shift argument (which depends on spatial correlation) gives the same answer all year. Per-month residual std only varies by about 2× (0.15 in Sep to 0.32 in Nov, SIC units); the mean flips sign (Jul −0.19, Nov +0.19).
- **The bias is a broad pull toward melt, not tied to pack ice.** In the 100-date DDIM run the bias is negative in every ice class, open water included (Oct–Dec: pack −0.13, MIZ −0.23, open water −0.10). That refutes the "ratchet at the k=+1 bound" hypothesis, which predicted open water would drift up. The cause is still unknown.
- **The worst months are the quiet ones.** With DDIM, Feb–Apr are worse than persistence (error vs persistence 1.08–1.31): the true change is about ±0.02, but the model predicts −0.07 to −0.12. Jun–Aug are good (0.25–0.53).

## Design decisions
- **Stop sweeping the noise schedule** for the seasonal problem; the evidence above puts the fault in the reverse chain.
- **Added DDIM as a sampling-only option.** Same 13 dates, same weights, error vs persistence: melt 0.41 → 0.37, cool 1.04 → 0.60. On 100 dates: melt 0.51, cool 0.68. It reduces the drift but doesn't remove it (cool mean −0.014 vs +0.105).
- **Added input perturbation (DDPM-IP, gamma=0.1) for a retrain**, `ddpm-titanfull-shif64-ip`. Everything else is identical to `titanfull-shif64`, and it samples with 1000-step DDPM. A clean 2×2 comparison (weights × sampler) needs that, with DDIM on both weight sets done afterwards from saved weights.
- **Rejected: relying on the unfolded loss.** It simulates one reverse step, but the drift builds over hundreds.
- **Deferred: use the UNet baseline to predict the conditional mean and diffuse only the remainder (CorrDiff style).** Revisit if the bias survives the input-perturbation retrain.
- **Deferred: ensemble calibration with DDIM.** `eta=0` puts all randomness in x_T and is expected to give too little spread. Measure spread-skill and rank histograms for eta 0, 0.5 and 1 (10 members × 20 dates) before trusting DDIM ensembles. Calibration is moot until the bias is fixed.

## Code design
- [models/ddpm.py](../../models/ddpm.py): `predict_x0()` now holds the network call and optional clamp for both samplers, so `p_sample` and `ddim_step` can't diverge. `ddim_timesteps()` always includes T−1 and 0. The final DDIM step uses `t_prev=-1` (alpha_bar = 1) and returns x0 itself.
- [config.py](../../config.py): `DDPMConfig.sampler` / `ddim_steps` / `ddim_eta` affect sampling only, so the weights work with either sampler. `TrainConfig.input_perturbation` is training-only (gated on `model.training`, like DOSE), so validation loss stays on clean inputs.
- Both `ConditionalDDPM` construction sites, [main.py](../../main.py) and `ddpm_forecaster` in [utils/sampling.py](../../utils/sampling.py), must pass the sampler settings. `ensemble.py` picks them up through `ddpm_forecaster`.
- **Known bug, not fixed:** with `residual_context=True`, `build_context` returns only the context residual and drops k and k−1 (3-channel input), contrary to its docstring. `ddpm-doubleC-rscale-shift128` was trained without k.
- **Known flaw, not fixed:** the varying-t check in `analysis_sampling` starts truncated chains from pure noise, so it doesn't measure what it claims.
