**Focus:** Diagnosed why the CARRA2-WEST samples had no spatial structure despite a low training loss, fixed the sampling pipeline, and added this diary skill.

## Theory

- **The loss was measuring nothing.** With linear betas 1e-4→0.02 over T=1000, `sqrt(ᾱ_t)` is 0.28 by t=500 and 0.006 by t=999, so for most of `t ~ U(0,T)` the input is already near-pure noise and ε-prediction collapses toward the identity map. Measured on the trained model: 0.45 at t=5 vs 0.0045 at t=900. The "0.0075" that looked like convergence was just the high-t bucket.
- CARRA2 amplifies this: 55% of water pixels are exactly 0, and over a locally constant patch ε-prediction reduces to an affine map a conv net fits in a handful of steps. That is the shortcut it found by step 5.
- **The eq. 7 posterior form and the eq. 11 direct form are the same function** — verified numerically (max abs diff 3e-8 at high t, ~1e-5 at low t). Reparameterising through x̂₀ imposes no new modelling assumption; it only stops simplifying one step early so x̂₀ is available to clamp.
- **Variance `β_t` vs `β̃_t`:** both appear in Ho et al. eq. 12. They differ only at low t (sd ratio 0.67 at t=1, 0.88 at t=5, 0.99 by t=100). `β_t` is justified for x₀ ~ N(0,I); SIC is near-binary and saturated, so `β̃_t` — the exact posterior variance in the deterministic-x₀ limit — is the better fit here.
- Minor: float32 cancellation in `1 − ᾱ_t` at small t makes `coef_x0[0] = 0.999834` rather than 1.0. Both parameterisations divide by that term, so neither is advantaged. float64 schedule would remove it.

## Design decisions

- **Land mask as a second input channel (+1 water / −1 land), not loss-masking alone.** `nan_to_num(x0, 0.0)` makes land a *legal* concentration, so masking the loss stops the penalty but never tells the network where the coast is — and unconditional sampling has no x₀ to derive a mask from at all.
- **Clamp x̂₀ to [−1,1] every reverse step.** Rejected keeping the direct ε form, which cannot express the clamp. The constraint is exact, not heuristic: SIC ∈ [0,1] by definition.
- **Kept `β̃_t` over `β_t`** after surfacing that it had changed. It was not required by the clamp and was the user's call.
- **`model.eval()` lives in `download_samples()`, not `__main__`** — so no other caller can bypass it. Dropout at 0.1 across 1000 reverse steps was a major contributor to the broken samples.
- **Deferred: undertraining.** See Open threads.

## Code design

- Schedule tensors (`betas`, `alphas`, `alphas_bar`, `alphas_bar_prev`, posterior coefficients) precomputed in `DDPM.__init__` — previously a `linspace`+`cumprod` per reverse step.
- `to_model_range` / `to_data_range` in [utils/dataloader.py](../../utils/dataloader.py) are the single home for the [0,1]↔[−1,1] convention. **Invariant:** datasets emit [−1,1]; `DDPM.sample()` returns [0,1] with land as NaN. Anything that bypasses these two functions breaks it.
- `CARRA2.finite_mask` is derived once from t=0 — verified the land geometry is identical across all 288 months. `FashionMNIST.finite_mask` is `None`, which makes `DDPM.model_input()` a pass-through and keeps `UnetSmall` 1-channel.
- **Old checkpoints are incompatible** — 1-channel, trained on unnormalized data. `results/Unet_CARRA2-siconc/model_final_batchsize2_t1000_epochs2_lr0.001.pth` will not load.
